# L0: physics layer (mass-conserving continuous CA, rules carried by matter)

## Design (arXiv check 2026-11-23)
- Transport: MaCE (Papadopoulos & Guichard, arXiv:2507.12306). Each cell splits its mass over its 3x3
  neighbourhood with weights exp(beta*A_q) normalised over that neighbourhood. Continuum limit: diffusion plus
  drift up the affinity gradient. Chosen over Flow-Lenia's reintegration tracking: local, cheaper, and the
  paper reports more solitons.
- Affinity: Lenia growth over a global kernel bank (FFT convolution), weighted per cell by the parameter vector P
  the matter carries (Flow-Lenia parameter localisation, arXiv:2212.07906, eq. 8).
- Incoming P: mass-weighted average (Flow-Lenia eq. 9) or sampling one source with probability proportional
  to its incoming mass (eq. 10, `-sample 1`).
- New: P mutates as matter moves (`-mut p -muts sd`), so the physics varies heritably inside the world.
  Neither paper has intrinsic heritable variation (Flow-Lenia mixes only; MaCE evolves in an outer ES loop).
- Mass is 64-bit fixed point, shares split by largest remainder: total mass is conserved exactly.
- Code: `src/l0/world.*`, `apps/oee_l0`, `tests/l0/test_l0.cpp` (10 checks: FFT vs direct convolution, exact
  conservation with and without mutation and in both mixing modes, determinism, no blending in sample mode).
  MSPD: `tools/mspd/l0_mspd.py` (state = density level x dominant kernel, from `-qdump`).
- CPU only: about 130 steps/s at 128x128 with 3 kernels on one core.

## Baseline: random physics (40 kernel banks, 128x128, 1000 steps)
32/40 localised (at least 80% of mass in 10% of cells), against MaCE's ~83% solitons. Of these, 28 move or change,
4 are static. Data: `docs/results/l0/survey_40.txt`.

## E1: mixing rule x mutation (6 banks, 128x128, 10k steps; mutation p 0.002, sd 0.05)
Types = distinct P vectors (0.05 bins) holding at least 1% of live mass at 10k. MSPD over the last 2048 steps.

| condition | types | type entropy (bits) | blobs | MSPD |

- Blending erases mutation (B vs A: +2.3 types); sampling keeps it (D vs C: +17.1 types, all 6 banks).
- More types is also what neutral drift produces. Selection is not shown. Next: neutral control (P carried and
  mutated as in D, but affinity uses a fixed shared P), compare diversity and direction of P change.
- MSPD about 0.001 everywhere (evolved Geb worlds: 0.011-0.036): no multi-scale organisation at this size/length.
